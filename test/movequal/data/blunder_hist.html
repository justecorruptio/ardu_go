250 games vs GnuGo L0 · 6,534 AI moves · scored by KataGo true winrate · NEW SHIP: pure-1000 × stable-stop-512 (918e0e9)
What changed: This corpus is played by the newly shipped iterations-arc engine (pure-1000 iterations × stable-leader stop K=512, commit 918e0e9) — +7.5pp vs the 10k-human and +3.9pp vs GnuGo L0 over the previous 400-iteration ship. Per-move quality vs the prior corpus: endgame mean loss 3.91→3.19%, midgame 8.77→8.35%, ≥25% blunders 649→603. All sections are on this one corpus.

AI winrate (KataGo, mover-agnostic AI perspective)
━ p50━ p60–p90━ p10–p40

The median game holds even through the opening and peaks at 64% around move 17 — the learned NN opening keeps ArduGo level or ahead well into the midgame. The median then slides to ~46% by move 35. The upper deciles run the other way (p70 70% by move 20) — games that clear the opening are usually already won; the lower deciles collapse into single digits. The distribution splits into a won-band and a lost-band inside the moves-10-to-25 window.
