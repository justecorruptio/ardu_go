Winrate trajectory — percentiles per move, 1,200 games

AI winrate (KataGo, mover-agnostic AI perspective)
━ p50 ━ p75 / p90 ━ p25 / p10

The median game peaks near 55% around move 6 (gnugo also plays a weak early opening), collapses from move ~10, and is effectively lost (p50 10%) by move 25. The p75 line — the games that get through the opening intact — holds ~65% deep into the midgame before the close-game endgame teeth bite after move ~45. p90 games are cleanly won and stay won. The whole loss distribution is decided in the moves-8-to-25 window.

KataGo review (1,200 games, 30,645 AI moves): true winrate loss per AI move (b6c96 net, 8 visits, black-perspective converted to mover).
